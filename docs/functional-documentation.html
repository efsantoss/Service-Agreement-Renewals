Introduction

This document describes what the Upcoming Renewals solution does, from a business point of view: which problem it solves, which rules it applies and what users see and do. It intentionally avoids implementation detail. How the solution is built is covered in the companion Technical Documentation.

Document status. Version 1.0 describes the delivered solution. Every rule below is implemented and covered by automated tests.

Audience

  • Business stakeholders who own the renewal process.
  • Account Managers and Sales Operations who use the solution daily.
  • Salesforce administrators who maintain configuration.

Business Context and Objectives

The problem

The customer manages Service Agreements in Salesforce. When an agreement reaches its end date without a renewal conversation, the customer risks losing recurring revenue or leaving a client without coverage. Today nothing in Salesforce tells the team which agreements are approaching expiration, and nobody is explicitly assigned to act on them.

Objectives

  • Automatically identify agreements that need renewal attention.
  • Give every qualifying agreement a clear owner and a due date.
  • Give the team one simple screen to review and manage upcoming renewals.
  • Never create noise: no duplicate work items and no silently dropped renewals.

Scope

In scope

Capability Description
Service Agreement record A custom object that stores the agreement and the data needed to manage its renewal.
Renewal identification A single, consistent definition of when an agreement requires attention.
Automatic Renewal Task A Task is created for the Account Manager when an agreement qualifies.
Upcoming Renewals screen A Lightning component that lists qualifying agreements and lets users mark renewal work as started.

Out of scope (first version)

  • Creating the renewed agreement itself (renewal opportunity or contract generation).
  • E-mail or push notifications.
  • Approval processes.
  • Business-day or holiday calendars (the business operates seven days a week).

Possible follow-ups are listed in Future Enhancements.

Users and Roles

Role Responsibility in this solution
Account Manager Receives the Renewal Task, reviews the agreement and starts the renewal conversation with the customer.
Renewal / Sales Operations lead Uses the Upcoming Renewals screen to see what is expiring, who owns it and how much value is at risk.
Salesforce administrator Maintains the renewal settings and assigns the permission set to users.

Business Rules

These rules are the single source of truth for the behavior of the solution.

ID Rule Definition
BR-01 Renewal attention criteria An agreement requires attention when all of these are true: Status is Active, Renewal Required is checked, and the End Date falls within the next 30 calendar days.
BR-02 The 30-day window The window runs from today through today + 30 days, both days included. Agreements that have already ended are not part of the window.
BR-03 Renewal Task creation When an agreement is created or updated and meets BR-01, a Renewal Task is created for the Account Manager.
BR-04 Task due date The Task is due 10 calendar days before the End Date. If that date has already passed (the agreement ends in fewer than 10 days), the Task is due today so that it is never created overdue.
BR-05 Task assignee The Task is assigned to the Account Manager. If the agreement has no Account Manager, or the Account Manager is inactive, the Task is assigned to the agreement owner (and, as a last resort, to the user who saved the agreement) so that no renewal is dropped.
BR-06 No duplicate Tasks An agreement has at most one Renewal Task. Editing the agreement again, in any way, never creates a second Task.
BR-07 Calendar days All calculations use calendar days. Weekends and holidays are counted like any other day. Month ends, year ends and leap years are handled correctly.
BR-08 Date consistency The End Date cannot be earlier than the Start Date.
BR-09 Renewal work status Each agreement tracks whether renewal work is Not Started or In Progress. Marking work as started does not change the agreement Status, so the agreement stays visible on the Upcoming Renewals screen.

Date examples

The due date is always the End Date minus 10 calendar days.

Scenario End Date Task due date
Month boundary Mar 5, 2027 Feb 23, 2027
Year boundary Jan 5, 2027 Dec 26, 2026

Service Agreement Data

The Service Agreement is a custom object. The fields below are the business view of the record. Technical names are listed in the Technical Documentation.

Field Type Required Purpose
Agreement Name Text Yes Human-friendly name of the agreement.
Account Lookup (Account) Yes The customer the agreement belongs to. An account with agreements cannot be deleted.
Start Date Date Yes First day the agreement is in force.
End Date Date Yes Last day of the agreement. Drives the renewal logic.
Status Picklist Yes Draft, Active, Expired or Cancelled.
Agreement Value Currency No Value of the agreement. Shown to help prioritize renewals.
Renewal Required Checkbox No Marks agreements that must be renewed. Unchecked agreements are ignored.
Account Manager Lookup (User) No The person responsible for the renewal and the default Task assignee.
Renewal Work Status Picklist Yes Not Started (default) or In Progress. See BR-09.
Renewal Task Id Text System Filled in automatically with the Id of the Renewal Task created for the agreement. It is how the system knows a Task already exists (BR-06). Users should not edit it.

Automatic Renewal Task

How it behaves

  1. A user, an integration or a data load saves a Service Agreement.
  2. Salesforce checks BR-01 for every saved agreement. This works for a single record and for large batches.
  3. For each qualifying agreement that does not already have a Renewal Task, one Task is created.
  4. Agreements that do not qualify are left untouched.

The Renewal Task

Task field Value
Subject Renewal: followed by the agreement name
Assigned To Account Manager, or the agreement owner as a fallback (BR-05)
Due Date End Date minus 10 calendar days (BR-04)
Related To The Service Agreement
Status / Priority Not Started / Normal

Edge cases

Situation Result
Agreement is edited after its Task was created No new Task (BR-06).
End Date is changed after the Task was created No new Task. The existing Task remains the single record of the renewal.
Agreement ends in fewer than 10 days Task is due today (BR-04).
Account Manager is empty or inactive Task goes to the agreement owner (BR-05).
Status is not Active, or Renewal Required is unchecked No Task.
End Date is outside the 30-day window No Task.

Upcoming Renewals Screen

Upcoming Renewals is a Lightning component that can be placed on a Home, App or Record page. It lists the agreements that meet BR-01.

What users see

Column Description
Account Customer name.
Agreement Agreement name, linking to the record.
End Date The agreement end date.
Days Remaining Calendar days from today to the End Date. Agreements ending within 10 days are highlighted in red.
Agreement Value Currency value of the agreement.
Account Manager Name of the responsible person.
Renewal Work Not Started or In Progress.

The list is ordered by the soonest End Date first, and by the highest value when dates are equal.

Marking renewal work as started

  1. The user selects one or more agreements in the list. The Start Renewal button is enabled only when something is selected.
  2. The user clicks Start Renewal. Agreements already In Progress are skipped.
  3. The selected agreements change to In Progress and the list refreshes.
  4. The user sees a confirmation message. If a record cannot be updated, a clear message explains why and the other records are still processed.

The screen also shows a friendly message when there is nothing to renew and when the data cannot be loaded, and a Refresh button reloads the list on demand. Starting renewal work also creates the Renewal Task if the agreement does not have one yet.

Security and Access

  • Users only see agreements they are allowed to see in Salesforce. The screen never bypasses sharing rules.
  • The screen enforces object and field access, including Agreement Value: a user without the required access receives a clear message instead of data.
  • Access is granted through a dedicated permission set, following the principle of least privilege.
  • Only users who can edit an agreement can mark its renewal work as started.

Assumptions

# Assumption
A1 The Service Agreement is a new custom object, because the standard Contract and Service Contract objects cannot store the required fields or control Status and End Date.
A2 "Within the next 30 days" means from today through today + 30, inclusive. Agreements that have already ended do not qualify.
A3 When the Account Manager is empty or inactive, the agreement owner receives the Task.
A4 One Renewal Task per agreement for its lifetime. Changing the End Date later does not create another Task.
A5 If the due date (End Date minus 10 days) is already in the past, the Task is due today.
A6 Marking renewal work as started is tracked on the agreement and does not change its Status.
A7 "Today" is based on the Salesforce user time zone settings.
A8 Service Agreements are visible to all internal users (Public Read/Write), so an Account Manager who is not the record owner can still see and update the agreement assigned to them.

Future Enhancements

  • Daily scheduled job. Agreements are evaluated when they are saved. A daily job would also catch agreements that enter the 30-day window without being edited.
  • Notifications (e-mail or in-app) for new and overdue Renewal Tasks.
  • Automatically move the Renewal Task to In Progress when renewal work is started.
  • Create a renewal opportunity or a draft renewed agreement.
  • Dashboards on renewal value at risk.

Glossary

Term Meaning
Service Agreement A contract that gives a customer an ongoing service for a period of time.
Renewal Task The Salesforce Task that tells someone to start the renewal of an agreement.
Calendar days Every day of the week counts, including weekends and holidays.
Account Manager The Salesforce user responsible for the customer relationship and for the renewal.
Permission set A Salesforce feature that grants a user access to objects, fields and code.
